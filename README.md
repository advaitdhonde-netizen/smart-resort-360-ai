Smart Resort 360

Smart Resort 360 is an AI-powered resort management and operational intelligence platform built to centralize and simplify day-to-day resort operations. The platform combines a modern management interface, Gemini-powered artificial intelligence, and an interactive 3D digital twin to provide a complete view of the resort and its operations.

The system covers reservations, guest management, rooms and villas, housekeeping, maintenance, restaurant operations, inventory, staff management, billing, feedback, and analytics. Different role-based interfaces allow administrators, managers, front-desk staff, housekeeping, maintenance teams, restaurant and inventory managers, staff members, and guests to access the functionality relevant to them.

AI-Powered Intelligence

Smart Resort 360 integrates Google Gemini as an operational intelligence layer. The AI Copilot allows staff to interact with resort data using natural language, ask questions about current operations, identify issues, and receive contextual insights. The platform also includes Issue Intelligence for analyzing operational problems and assisting staff with decision-making.

Interactive 3D Digital Twin

The platform features an immersive 3D representation of the resort, providing an interactive view of areas such as guest rooms, villas, the swimming pool, restaurant, spa, and other resort facilities. This digital twin serves as the visual centerpiece of the platform while connecting the physical resort environment with its operational data.

Technology

Frontend: React, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS
Backend: Python, FastAPI
AI: Google Gemini
Deployment: Vercel
Version Control: GitHub

Smart Resort 360 is designed to demonstrate how AI, modern web technologies, and immersive visualization can be combined to create a unified platform for managing and understanding resort operations.
